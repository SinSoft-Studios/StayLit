Privacy Policy – StayLit

Effective date: October 27, 2026  |  Last updated: October 27, 2026

SinSoft Studios develops StayLit, an Android utility that temporarily keeps your screen awake. For privacy questions, contact sinsoftstudios@gmail.com.

Planned advertising: We plan to add Google AdMob banner ads. The current app does not request ads or include the advertising SDK. The advertising practices and privacy controls below apply only to future versions with advertising enabled.

Data and Local Storage

No account is required. The current app works offline and has no ads, analytics, or crash-reporting services. StayLit does not read or record the content of your screen or other apps.

Your selected duration, appearance, language, setup preferences, and information needed to manage sessions and restore the screen timeout are stored on your device. We do not receive this data or provide cloud sync. App settings and recovery data are excluded from Android backup and device transfer.

Advertising in Future Versions

Ad-supported versions will use Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner ads. Depending on SDK configuration, consent, and device settings, Google and participating advertising providers may collect and share:

  • IP address, which can indicate approximate location.
  • Advertising ID and other available device or account identifiers.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, impressions, and taps.
  • Technical diagnostics and consent choices.

This supports ad delivery, measurement, service performance, and fraud prevention, and personalized advertising where permitted and consented to. Advertising may use device identifiers and local storage; pages opened from ads may use cookies. We will not send your saved settings, screen contents, or support emails as ad targeting data. We may receive ad performance and revenue reports.

Google receives advertising information directly through its SDK. See Google's SDK data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. Other advertising providers and sites opened from ads have their own policies; review the provider list in the consent message where shown.

Advertising Choices

Before requesting ads, ad-supported versions will use a consent-management flow where required, including in the EEA (including Norway), the UK, and Switzerland. Personalized ads will be enabled only where permitted and with required consent. Using StayLit is not consent to personalized advertising.

Where required, Privacy choices in Settings will let you review, refuse, or withdraw choices. Refusing personalization will not disable the timer. Depending on your choices, you may receive non-personalized or limited ads, or no ads. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing for purposes such as reporting and fraud prevention. Android also provides advertising-ID controls.

Where applicable law provides an opt-out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising, use Privacy choices or contact us. Advertising disclosures may fall within these definitions even without payment for data.

Permissions and Screen Settings

Modify system settings access lets StayLit read and temporarily change your screen inactivity timeout so the screen stays on across apps. It saves the original setting and attempts to restore it when you stop, the timer ends, or the screen turns off. StayLit does not unlock your device.

If the app is force-stopped, uninstalled, or loses access, restoration may be interrupted. Reopen StayLit to retry cleanup, or restore the timeout in Android Display settings.

Notifications show the countdown, reminders, and session controls. A foreground service runs an active session. Startup and app-update handling only attempt cleanup; they do not restart a session. Permissions can be managed in Android settings.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and retain it only as long as needed for support, follow-up, or legal obligations. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver and store it. We do not exchange support correspondence or your saved app settings for advertising payments.

Where European data-protection law applies, we rely on consent for advertising personalization and device storage where required, legitimate interests in answering support requests and protecting our services, and legal obligations where applicable. Consent requirements take priority.

Deletion and Your Choices

Local data remains until replaced or removed. You can clear app storage or uninstall StayLit to remove it. Stop any active session first and check your screen timeout, since deleting app data also removes restoration information.

We have no server copy of your app settings. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy of your information, withdraw consent, and complain to your data-protection authority.

Google and other advertising providers determine retention under their policies. See Google's retention information. Uninstalling or withdrawing consent does not erase information already held by those providers; use their privacy request options or contact us for help. Withdrawal applies to future processing.

Security and Changes

Local data is stored in app-private storage and relies on your device's security. We use reasonable safeguards for support correspondence. Google documents encrypted transmission for Mobile Ads SDK data. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Service providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Google describes its safeguards in its data transfer frameworks.

We will update this policy if our data practices change and revise the date above. Questions or requests: sinsoftstudios@gmail.com.
